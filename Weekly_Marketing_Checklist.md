# Weekly Marketing Action Checklist for StormShift Labs

## Daily Tasks (Monday - Friday)
### Morning (30 minutes)
- [ ] Check and respond to inquiries (email, LinkedIn, website)
- [ ] Send 5 personalized outreach emails to prospects
- [ ] Connect with 10 new people on LinkedIn
- [ ] Comment on 5 relevant LinkedIn posts
- [ ] Share 1 piece of valuable content on LinkedIn

### Evening (15 minutes)
- [ ] Follow up with any pending conversations
- [ ] Schedule tomorrow's social media posts
- [ ] Review daily metrics (website traffic, email opens)

---

## Monday - Planning & Content
- [ ] Review last week's metrics
- [ ] Plan this week's content topics
- [ ] Write and schedule week's social media posts
- [ ] Update project pipeline and CRM
- [ ] Send weekly newsletter (if applicable)

## Tuesday - Outreach Focus
- [ ] Research 10 new prospect companies
- [ ] Personalize outreach templates
- [ ] Send LinkedIn connection requests (20)
- [ ] Follow up with last week's cold emails
- [ ] Join and participate in 1 online community discussion

## Wednesday - Content Creation
- [ ] Write 1 blog post (or work on longer piece)
- [ ] Create 1 piece of visual content (infographic, chart)
- [ ] Record 1 video (even if just for practice)
- [ ] Update website with new content
- [ ] Repurpose content for different platforms

## Thursday - Networking & Partnerships
- [ ] Reach out to 2 potential partners
- [ ] Schedule coffee chats or calls
- [ ] Attend virtual networking event (if available)
- [ ] Engage in industry forums/communities
- [ ] Request testimonials from satisfied contacts

## Friday - Analysis & Optimization
- [ ] Review week's metrics and KPIs
- [ ] A/B test email subject lines or CTAs
- [ ] Update prospect database
- [ ] Plan next week's priorities
- [ ] Celebrate wins and learn from challenges

## Time Investment Guide

### Minimum Viable Marketing (2 hours/day)
- 30 min: Email and inquiry responses
- 30 min: Outreach and prospecting
- 30 min: Content creation or curation
- 30 min: Social media engagement

### Growth Mode (4 hours/day)
- 1 hour: Email and inquiry responses
- 1 hour: Outreach and prospecting
- 1 hour: Content creation
- 30 min: Social media engagement
- 30 min: Analytics and optimization

### Aggressive Growth (6+ hours/day)
- All of the above plus:
- 1 hour: Paid advertising management
- 1 hour: Partnership development
- Additional content creation
- Speaking and event participation
