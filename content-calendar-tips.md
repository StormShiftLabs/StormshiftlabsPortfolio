# Tips for Presenting Your Content Calendar to Evergreen Calibration

## Before the Meeting

### 1. Prepare Your Materials
- Print physical copies of the calendar (people like to see/touch proposals)
- Have digital version ready on tablet/laptop
- Bring examples of similar successful campaigns
- Have your existing videos ready to show

### 2. Know Your Numbers
- Research their current Instagram engagement rates
- Note their follower count and growth rate
- Identify what their competitors are doing well
- Have rough pricing ready but be flexible

## During the Presentation

### 1. Start with the Problem
"We've noticed your current social media could better reflect the high-quality work Evergreen does..."
- Point out specific missed opportunities (without being too critical)
- Show how video content performs better in automotive space
- Emphasize the untapped potential

### 2. Focus on Value, Not Just Posts
Instead of: "We'll post 3 times a week"
Say: "We'll build brand authority through consistent, professional content"

### 3. Use Your Existing Work as Proof
- Show how the videos you've already created can be repurposed
- Demonstrate the quality difference between your work and current posts
- This shows immediate value - you already have content ready to go

### 4. Make it Personal
"As family, we understand Evergreen's values and can authentically represent the brand"
- Emphasize faster communication
- Highlight personal investment in success
- Mention flexibility that comes with family business

### 5. Address His Concerns
Since he said "no current need for social media," focus on:
- ROI and lead generation potential
- Building authority for future growth
- Creating assets that have long-term value
- Minimal time investment required from him

## Key Selling Points to Emphasize

### 1. Multiple Content from Single Shoot
"From the 5 videos we've already created, we can generate 30+ pieces of content"
- Shows efficiency
- Maximizes their existing investment
- Proves you can do more with less

### 2. Industry-Specific Knowledge
"We understand ADAS technology and can create educational content that positions Evergreen as the expert"
- Not just pretty pictures, but valuable information
- Builds trust with potential customers
- Sets them apart from competitors

### 3. Video-First Strategy
"Video content gets 1200% more shares than text and images combined"
- Play to your strengths
- Modern approach that competitors aren't doing
- Higher engagement rates

### 4. Measurable Results
"We'll provide monthly reports showing exactly what's working"
- Takes the guesswork out
- Shows professional approach
- Allows for optimization

## Pricing Strategy Suggestions

### Option 1: Performance-Based Trial
- First month at reduced rate
- If specific metrics are hit, continue at full rate
- Shows confidence in your abilities
- Lower risk for them

### Option 2: Package Deal
- Bundle social media with future video work
- Creates ongoing relationship
- Better value for both parties
- Ensures consistent content creation

### Option 3: Start Small, Scale Up
- Begin with 2 posts per week + stories
- Add more as you prove value
- Lower initial investment
- Room to grow

## Objection Handling

### "We don't need social media right now"
**Response**: "Think of it as building a digital asset. Every post, video, and follower adds value to your business. When you do need it, you'll already have an established presence."

### "The current company is fine"
**Response**: "Let's do a one-month trial. If we don't significantly outperform them, you can stick with them. But look at what we've already created vs. what they're posting."

### "It's too expensive"
**Response**: "Consider that we're not just posting - we're creating valuable video content that can be used across all marketing channels. Plus, as family, we're invested in finding a price that works."

### "We're too busy"
**Response**: "That's exactly why you need us. We handle everything - you just approve the calendar once a month. 15 minutes of your time per month maximum."

## Software Recommendations

### For Now: Keep It Simple
1. **Start with native Instagram scheduling** (Free)
   - Instagram Creator Studio
   - Simple and built-in

2. **Later.com** (Free plan available)
   - Visual calendar
   - Basic analytics
   - Easy to learn

3. **Canva** (For graphics)
   - Templates for consistent branding
   - Easy to create quote cards, announcements

### Don't overwhelm them with tech:
- You handle the tools
- They just see results
- Can always upgrade later

## Final Presentation Tips

1. **Be confident but not pushy** - You have real value to offer
2. **Show enthusiasm** - You're excited about the possibilities
3. **Listen to their concerns** - Address specific needs
4. **Leave something tangible** - Printed calendar they can review
5. **Set clear next steps** - Don't leave it open-ended

## The Closer

"Look, you're already paying for social media that's underperforming. We've already created professional content that's not being utilized. Give us one month to show you what family members who actually understand and care about your business can do. Worst case, you go back to the other company with some great content in the bank. Best case, you save money and get better results."

## Remember

- You have unique advantages (family connection, existing content, video expertise)
- You're not just another social media company
- You've already proven your quality with the videos
- This is a natural extension of work you're already doing
- Confidence is key - you CAN do better than the current company

Good luck! You've got this! 💪
