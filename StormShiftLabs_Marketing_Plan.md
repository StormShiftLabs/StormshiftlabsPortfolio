## 11. Marketing Message Templates

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"StormShift Labs helps [target audience] solve [specific problem] through [your solution]. Unlike [competitors], we [unique differentiator], resulting in [key benefit]."

### Email Outreach Template
```
Subject: Quick question about [Company's] tech challenges

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about their company]. Many businesses in [industry] struggle with [common problem].

At StormShift Labs, we've helped companies like yours [specific outcome]. For example, [brief case study or statistic].

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute call to discuss how we might help [Company] achieve similar results?

Best regards,
[Your name]
```

### LinkedIn Connection Request
```
Hi [Name], I see we're both in the [industry] space. I'd love to connect and share insights on [relevant topic]. Looking forward to connecting!
```

### Follow-Up Email
```
Subject: Following up - [Company] tech solutions

Hi [Name],

I wanted to follow up on my previous email about helping [Company] with [specific challenge].

I understand you're busy, so I'll keep this brief. We recently helped [similar company] achieve [specific result] in just [timeframe].

If this isn't a priority right now, I completely understand. Would it make sense to reconnect in [timeframe]?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trying everything at once** - Focus on 2-3 channels initially
2. **Giving up too soon** - Marketing takes 3-6 months to show results
3. **Not tracking metrics** - You can't improve what you don't measure
4. **Ignoring existing clients** - Referrals are your best source of new business
5. **Being too salesy** - Provide value first, sell second
6. **Inconsistent messaging** - Keep your brand voice consistent
7. **Neglecting follow-up** - 80% of sales require 5+ touchpoints

## Final Thoughts

Remember, marketing is a marathon, not a sprint. The key to success is:

1. **Consistency** - Show up every day
2. **Value-First** - Always lead with value
3. **Persistence** - Don't give up after a few weeks
4. **Testing** - Try different approaches and measure results
5. **Authenticity** - Be genuine in your communications

Start with the immediate actions, build momentum, and scale what works. You don't need a huge budget to start marketing effectively - you need strategy, consistency, and dedication.

**Your first client is just 90 days of consistent effort away!**
