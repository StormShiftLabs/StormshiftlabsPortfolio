## Content Creation Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Color Palette**: Professional greens (brand colors), whites, blacks, automotive metallics
- **Photo Style**: Clean, well-lit, professional automotive photography
- **Video Style**: Dynamic, informative, showcasing precision and expertise
- **Graphics**: Modern, clean, technical but accessible

### Caption Strategy
- **Hook**: Start with a question or interesting fact
- **Value**: Provide educational or entertaining content
- **CTA**: Include clear call-to-action (visit, call, book)
- **Length**: Mix of short (1-2 lines) and medium (3-5 lines) captions

### Hashtag Strategy
- **Mix**: 30% branded, 40% industry, 30% local/community
- **Number**: 15-20 per feed post, 5-8 per reel
- **Research**: Monitor competitor and industry leader hashtags
